Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Zishu Discord bot (“Zishu” or the “Bot”) processes information when it sends YouTube livestream notifications to Discord members.

1. Information the Bot may process

To identify members of a participating server and send them direct messages, the Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, and server membership information. To prepare notifications, it may process publicly available YouTube information such as channel names, livestream titles, and livestream status. Operational logs or error details may also be generated when the Bot runs.

2. How information is used

This information is used to identify the intended Discord recipients, deliver livestream notifications, operate and secure the Bot, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support requests.

3. Direct messages

When a notification is triggered, Zishu is configured to send a direct message to every member of the participating Discord server. Whether a message is successfully delivered depends on Discord settings and technical restrictions. Server administrators should notify members about this behaviour before enabling the Bot. You can adjust your Discord privacy settings, block the Bot, or leave the server to limit messages.

4. Sharing and service providers

The Bot does not need to sell personal information to provide its stated function. Information may be processed by Discord, YouTube, and the hosting or infrastructure providers actually used to operate the Bot, as necessary for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. Those services have their own privacy policies.

5. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the Bot’s operation, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. Actual retention depends on the Bot’s hosting, database, and logging configuration. Reasonable safeguards should be used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and requests

You can limit direct messages through your Discord privacy settings, block the Bot, or leave the participating server. For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by the Bot operator, contact the operator through the GitHub support page.

7. Children

The Bot is not designed to collect information directly from children. Users must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and comply with applicable law.

8. Changes

This Policy may be updated as the Bot changes. Updates will be posted here with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Bot operator through the GitHub support page.
