What Zishu does

When a YouTube livestream notification is triggered, Zishu is configured to send a direct message to every member of the participating Discord server. Delivery can be affected by Discord privacy settings, blocked messages, rate limits, outages, and other technical limitations.

Server administrators: Please inform your members that the bot sends automated direct messages to server members before enabling it. Use the bot only where recipients have been appropriately informed and the messaging is permitted by Discord's rules.
